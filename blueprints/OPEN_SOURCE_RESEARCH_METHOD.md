# Blueprint: Open-Source Research Method

**Methodology proposal v0.1.**

## Purpose
A repeatable, proportionate method for investigating public-interest questions using lawfully accessible information. This supports accountability reporting, not profiling private people or enabling targeting.

## Research sequence
1. **Question:** Define a narrow public-interest question, scope, and time window.
2. **Source map:** Identify official records, primary documents, public datasets, independent reporting, and relevant expertise.
3. **Collection plan:** Record permitted access methods, expected fields, rights status, and retention needs before collection.
4. **Observation:** Capture only what is necessary; record URL, publisher, observed time, method, language, and limitations.
5. **Preservation:** Store metadata and digests by default; retain full content only with a rights basis and safety review.
6. **Verification:** Check origin, context, date, integrity, and independent corroboration.
7. **Analysis:** Separate observation from inference; document alternatives and missing data.
8. **Review:** Independent peer review, harm review, and subject response where appropriate.
9. **Publication:** Explain method and uncertainty; disclose material AI assistance; cite primary evidence.
10. **Correction:** Maintain visible correction and update history.

## Source independence
Two URLs are not necessarily two independent sources. Check whether they quote the same wire story or press release, derive from one dataset, repeat an unattributed post, share ownership/authorship, or rely on the same witness or official statement. Record dependencies so repetition is not mistaken for corroboration.

## AI-assisted research protocol
AI may help summarize public documents, suggest search terms, classify metadata, or translate passages. It is not a source.
- Keep original source and generated output separate.
- Record tool/version and task when material.
- Verify quotations, dates, legal references, numbers, and translations against originals.
- Do not submit confidential source data or sensitive unpublished material to external models without approved security and privacy assessment.
- Never publish synthetic citations, generated quotes, inferred identities, or unsupported claims.
- A human editor remains accountable for every published assertion.

## Method record
Document question and scope; search terms and date range; public repositories consulted; collection date/timezone; tool names/versions; inclusion/exclusion criteria; known gaps and failed searches; reproducible steps; and independent reviewer.

## Prohibited uses
No doxxing, stalking, harassment, discriminatory profiling, credential theft, access-control bypass, covert tracking of private individuals, or publication of sensitive personal data without a compelling lawful basis and rigorous review.
