# Blueprint: Regional Adaptation Kit

**Purpose:** Help local journalism communities adapt the global manual without assuming one country's law, language, newsroom structure, or security environment applies everywhere.

## Required local profile
For each country, state, province, or region, maintain a reviewed profile containing:
- Jurisdiction name and stable identifier.
- Official language(s), common working languages, scripts, and accessibility needs.
- Current access-to-information / public-records laws and official request channels.
- Oversight, appeal, and complaint bodies.
- Current source-protection and journalist-protection resources.
- Relevant privacy, data-protection, copyright, defamation, and evidence rules.
- Local emergency and journalist-support organizations.
- Trusted digital-security support and referral process.
- Known limitations: inaccessible portals, paywalls, language barriers, connectivity, censorship, or conflict risks.
- Reviewer roles and last-checked date.

## Local validation checklist
- [ ] Reviewed by working journalists in the region.
- [ ] Reviewed by a local media-law expert or qualified legal organization.
- [ ] Reviewed by a digital-security practitioner familiar with local threats.
- [ ] Reviewed by language communities and accessibility specialists.
- [ ] Includes urban, rural, Indigenous / minority, and low-connectivity perspectives where relevant.
- [ ] Official source links were checked on the stated review date.
- [ ] Deadlines, fees, exemptions, and appeal routes were checked against current official sources.
- [ ] No imported legal advice is presented as locally applicable.
- [ ] Translation is reviewed and original-language terminology retained.
- [ ] A correction and update owner is named by role.

## Local public-records appendix
For each applicable access-to-information law, record:
- Official title and citation.
- Scope of covered public bodies.
- Who may request records.
- Request method and required details.
- Response deadlines and extension rules.
- Fees and waiver options.
- Exemptions and partial-release rules.
- Appeal / review route and deadlines.
- Oversight body and official guidance URL.
- Date verified and reviewer role.

## Local safety appendix
- Threat patterns relevant to reporters and sources.
- Legal risks and limits on source confidentiality.
- Trusted referral organizations.
- Safe handling practices appropriate to the local context.
- Emergency escalation options.
- Stop-work triggers.
- What this project cannot safely support.

## Governance
Local profiles should be maintained by a plural group, not a single unreviewed editor. Disclose funding and conflicts of interest. Provide a public correction route. Do not collect a public directory of individual journalists or confidential sources. Regional profiles are informational and must clearly state that laws and conditions change.
