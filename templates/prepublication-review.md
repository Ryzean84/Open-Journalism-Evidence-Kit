# Pre-publication Review — Checklist

## Accuracy and evidence
- [ ] Every material factual claim maps to evidence.
- [ ] Primary sources were sought and identified where available.
- [ ] Corroboration is independent, not circular repetition.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence and alternative explanations were considered.
- [ ] Dates, locations, names, quantities, and quotations were checked.
- [ ] Translations are labeled and reviewed; originals remain distinct.
- [ ] Facts, attributed claims, analysis, and inference are distinguished.
- [ ] Limitations and uncertainty are stated plainly.

## Fairness and response
- [ ] Subjects received a fair opportunity to respond where appropriate and safe.
- [ ] Responses are represented accurately and in context.
- [ ] Guilt, motive, and intent are not inferred beyond evidence.
- [ ] Headlines, captions, graphics, and social posts meet the same standard.

## Safety, privacy, and rights
- [ ] Confidential source identities are absent from public files and metadata.
- [ ] Personal data is necessary, proportionate, and reviewed under applicable law.
- [ ] Images, maps, and documents do not expose vulnerable people or sensitive locations.
- [ ] Copyright, licenses, terms, and reuse permissions were reviewed.
- [ ] Redactions cannot be trivially reversed.
- [ ] Publication does not enable harassment, targeting, or vigilantism.
- [ ] Risk assessment and stop-work decision are documented.

## Transparency and reproducibility
- [ ] Methodology and material tools are disclosed sufficiently for review.
- [ ] Material AI assistance is disclosed and independently human-verified.
- [ ] Links, archive references, and evidence-package checks were tested.
- [ ] Corrections contact and update policy are visible.
- [ ] Public datasets have a data dictionary and rights statement.
