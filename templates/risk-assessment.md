## Operational boundaries
- No unauthorized access, impersonation, stalking, doxxing, or harassment.
- No public storage of source identities, credentials, private contact details, or sensitive personal data.
- No promise of anonymity without specialist review of the threat model and local legal context.
- No publication of precise locations where it could expose a vulnerable person.
- OJEK is not a confidential dropbox.

## Stop-work triggers
Pause and seek qualified help if a source or bystander faces credible retaliation risk; the work requires unauthorized access; sensitive material cannot be protected; a legal demand, threat, detention, device compromise, or targeted harassment occurs; or foreseeable harm outweighs public benefit.
